# Research

A concise overview of my research, projects and publication profiles.

My research asks a distributional question:

> **When governments set national agricultural, climate and land-use targets, which farms, production systems and places actually carry the adjustment?**

I use spatial, economic and behavioural modelling to connect national pathways with the local realities through which they must be implemented.

## Current work

**[GOBLIN-Spatial](../projects/goblin-spatial/)**\
Spatial foresight for where nationally coherent agricultural transitions fall across Ireland and whether future land-use requirements can fit.

**[BioLand-US](../projects/bioland-us/)**\
A U.S. implementation model examining how compatible land and voluntary contractual access constrain prospective perennial-biomass mobilisation.

**[IFT-BioSim](../projects/ift-biosim/)**\
A farm-level framework for studying heterogeneous opportunity costs, participation and the distribution of transition costs.

## Methods

My work uses econometrics, spatial modelling, optimisation, synthetic populations and microsimulation, implemented mainly in Python, R, Stata, GIS and SQL.
