# Public source ledger

This ledger records public evidence used for the draft. It contains no confidential or unpublished research material.

## Programme design and implementation
- 24H+ official home page. Defines 24H+ as a production-led transformation programme focused on productivity, capital utilisation, value addition and multiple shifts: https://24hplus.gov.gh/
- 24H+ Vision and Objectives. Authority mandate includes monitoring performance targets and publishing scorecards: https://24hplus.gov.gh/vision-and-objectives/
- 24H+ full programme document. National outcome indicators include GDP growth, import dependency, unemployment, youth unemployment, jobs by sector, vulnerable employment and manufacturing share of GDP: https://24hplus.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Full-Programme_24Hplus_vF_202507.pdf
- Ghana 2026 Budget Statement. Calls 24H+ a productivity revolution and targets more than 1.7 million jobs by 2028: https://mofep.gov.gh/sites/default/files/budget-statements/2026-Budget-Statement-and-Economic-Policy.pdf
- 24H+ Authority, 5 June 2026. Reiterates 1.7 million productive-jobs target for 2028: https://24hplus.gov.gh/24-hour-economy-on-track-to-create-1-7-million-jobs-by-2028-goosie-tanoh/
- National Petroleum Authority, 12 May 2026. Pilot phase covers 268 filling stations, eight depots and two refineries in four regions: https://npa.gov.gh/npa-24-hr-economy-authority-launch-pilot-phase-of-24-hr-economy-in-petroleum-downstream-sector/
- Ghana News Agency, 17 July 2026. Secretariat reported 33 manufacturing companies under one-to-three-shift systems, US$5.5bn in project agreements and more than 160,000 projected jobs. These are reported pipeline figures, not treated as realised outcomes: https://gna.org.gh/2026/07/24-hour-economy-secures-5-5bn-investment-deals-160000-jobs-in-the-offing/
- Ghana News Agency, 28 August 2026. Businesses seeking incentives will undergo performance assessment tied to jobs, investment and productivity; beneficiary firms are intended to be publicly registered and annual reports used to assess targets: https://gna.org.gh/2026/08/businesses-seeking-24-hour-economy-incentives-to-undergo-performance-assessment/
- Ghana News Agency, 21 August 2026. Secretariat says policy aims to remove supply-chain and financing constraints and raise factory capacity utilisation: https://gna.org.gh/2026/08/govt-targets-higher-industrial-capacity-investment-to-boost-business-growth/

## Productivity and constraints
- Ghana Statistical Service, Productivity Statistics Report. Defines labour and multifactor productivity and reports capital deepening growing about twice as fast as labour productivity since 2010: https://statsghana.gov.gh/gssmain/fileUpload/pressrelease/Productivity%20Statistics%20Report_Final_28th%20February,%202025.pdf
- Yakubu Abdul-Salam (2024), Socio-Economic Planning Sciences 95, 102017. Dynamic CGE comparison of a 24-hour-economy scenario with business as usual; reports real GDP 31.71% higher after ten years and more than 3 million jobs within five years under the modelled policy scenario. Treated as ex ante modelling, not observed programme impact: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.seps.2024.102017
- IMF, Ghana's Energy Sector: From Fiscal Vulnerability to a Platform for Sustained Growth, 2026. Says Ghana's industrial ambitions, including 24H+, require a step change in reliability, affordability and financial sustainability of electricity: https://www.elibrary.imf.org/view/journals/018/2026/086/article-A001-en.xml
- World Bank indicator from Enterprise Surveys, Ghana 2023. 73.76% of firms experienced electrical outages: https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/IC.ELC.OUTG.ZS?locations=GH

## Editorial and verification decisions
- The reader-supplied revision of 9 October 2026 was used as the structural basis for this version.
- The supplied front matter pointed to a PNG while the actual lead asset is SVG; the image field is corrected to the SVG.
- The supplied endnote named Abdul-Salam's journal as Structural Change and Economic Dynamics; the verified publication is Socio-Economic Planning Sciences 95, 102017.
- "Natural comparison group" was softened to "potential evaluation opportunity": staggered participation does not automatically create a valid control group because selection, different pre-trends and spillovers can bias the comparison.
- "Data are not currently being collected" was narrowed to "not yet publicly reported at that level"; the public evidence does not establish what confidential firm-level data the Authority already holds.
- The public article keeps sources inline; the source ledger remains non-rendered.
- No existing EKO Perspectives post covers Ghana's 24-hour economy.
- Semrush keyword-volume and keyword-difficulty data remain unavailable because the connected account has insufficient API units. No search-volume or difficulty figures are inferred.
