# Public source ledger

This ledger records public evidence used for the draft. It contains no confidential or unpublished research material.

## Programme design and implementation
- 24H+ official home page. Defines 24H+ as a production-led transformation programme focused on productivity, capital utilisation, value addition and multiple shifts: https://24hplus.gov.gh/
- 24H+ Vision and Objectives. Authority mandate includes monitoring performance targets and publishing scorecards: https://24hplus.gov.gh/vision-and-objectives/
- 24H+ full programme document. National outcome indicators include GDP growth, import dependency, unemployment, youth unemployment, jobs by sector, vulnerable employment and manufacturing share of GDP: https://24hplus.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Full-Programme_24Hplus_vF_202507.pdf
- Ghana 2026 Budget Statement. Calls 24H+ a productivity revolution and targets more than 1.7 million jobs by 2028: https://mofep.gov.gh/sites/default/files/budget-statements/2026-Budget-Statement-and-Economic-Policy.pdf
- National Petroleum Authority, 12 May 2026. Pilot phase covers 268 filling stations, eight depots and two refineries in four regions: https://npa.gov.gh/npa-24-hr-economy-authority-launch-pilot-phase-of-24-hr-economy-in-petroleum-downstream-sector/
- Ghana News Agency, 17 July 2026. Secretariat reported 33 manufacturing companies under one-to-three-shift systems, US$5.5bn in project agreements and more than 160,000 projected jobs. These are reported pipeline figures, not treated as realised outcomes: https://gna.org.gh/2026/07/24-hour-economy-secures-5-5bn-investment-deals-160000-jobs-in-the-offing/
- Ghana News Agency, 28 August 2026. Businesses seeking incentives will undergo performance assessment tied to jobs, investment and productivity: https://gna.org.gh/2026/08/businesses-seeking-24-hour-economy-incentives-to-undergo-performance-assessment/

## Productivity, labour market and macro context
- Ghana Statistical Service, Productivity Statistics Report. Defines labour and multifactor productivity; reports that capital deepening since 2010 has grown about twice as fast as labour productivity and that structural transformation has been slow: https://statsghana.gov.gh/gssmain/fileUpload/pressrelease/Productivity%20Statistics%20Report_Final_28th%20February,%202025.pdf
- Ghana Statistical Service, youth release, 2026. National unemployment averaged 12.8% in Q1-Q3 2025; youth unemployment aged 15-35 averaged 21.9%; nearly two million youth were NEET in Q3: https://www.statsghana.gov.gh/news-and-events/press-releases/more-than-one-third-of-ghanas-population-is-youth
- Ghana Statistical Service, Q2 2026 GDP highlights. Real GDP growth 6.0%: https://www.statsghana.gov.gh/highlights/gdp-performance

## Counterfactual evidence and constraints
- Yakubu Abdul-Salam (2024), Socio-Economic Planning Sciences 95, 102017. Dynamic CGE comparison of 24-hour-economy and business-as-usual scenarios; reports substantial GDP, investment and labour-demand gains under the policy scenario. Treated as ex ante modelling, not observed programme impact: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.seps.2024.102017
- IMF, Ghana's Energy Sector: From Fiscal Vulnerability to a Platform for Sustained Growth, 2026. States 24H+ depends on a step change in reliability, affordability and financial sustainability of electricity: https://www.elibrary.imf.org/view/journals/018/2026/086/article-A001-en.xml
- World Bank indicator from Enterprise Surveys, Ghana 2023. 73.76% of firms experienced electrical outages: https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/IC.ELC.OUTG.ZS?locations=GH

## Editorial and search decisions
- Search-result review on 9 October 2026 found the generic angle "24-hour economy is about productivity, not longer hours" already well covered by official pages and recent commentary. The draft therefore uses a narrower measurement/counterfactual angle.
- Semrush keyword-volume and difficulty data were unavailable because the connected account currently has insufficient API units. No search-volume or keyword-difficulty numbers are inferred.
- No existing EKO Perspectives post covers Ghana's 24-hour economy, so there is no direct topic duplication.
