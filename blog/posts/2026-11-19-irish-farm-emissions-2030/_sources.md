## Model piece (workflow Stage 4, 1990 to 2015 rule)

- **Model:** Alan Matthews, "Greenhouse gas emission targets and Irish agriculture", CAP Reform blog (capreform.eu), 6 December 2015. Inside the 1990-2015 window (its last month). Same author as the CAP post's model; swap for a different writer if Elvis prefers variety.
- **Its structure, which this post borrows:**
  1. Hook: the week's news about Irish agricultural emissions (Taoiseach's COP21 remarks) and what was actually said.
  2. "The 2020 target": EPA data against the target path, with the author's own chart from EPA figures; which sectors dominate.
  3. "Targets to 2030": the EPA projections and how far short they fall.
  4. "Setting national targets": what a fair target is; efficiency versus equity; flexibility.
- **How this post uses it:** (1) hook: the 2025 figures (cattle down, fertiliser and milk up, flat); (2) "The 2025 commitment" with an original chart from EPA data, plus the ceiling/inventory revision; (3) "Targets to 2030": EPA projections and Climate Council; (4) "What a fair target looks like": shifted from fairness between countries to fairness between farms. No sentences reused.

## Claims
- 2025 agriculture 20.40 Mt CO2eq, -0.2% on 2024; cattle -3.3%; dairy cows -0.9%; milk per cow +5.6%; milk production +4.8%; N fertiliser +12.7% to 349.9 kt; N2O +5.0%; 38.7% of national total excl. LULUCF: EPA Provisional GHG Emissions 1990-2025 (July 2026), pp. 3, 13-14. Primary.
- Series 2018-2025 (kt): 21,402; 21,284; 21,588; 21,968; 21,780; 20,720; 20,436; 20,398: EPA report Table A.1 and Tables 2-3 (2021-25 and 2018 cross-check exactly).
- -4.7% since 2018; indicative ~10% by 2025 and ~25% by 2030: EPA Table 3 footnote; Climate Council annual review press release 9 Sept 2026 ("less than half of the 10% reduction commitment").
- Ceiling 2021-25: 105.301 of 106.000 Mt used, 99.3%; "direct comparison ... no longer viable"; 2023 inventory science update; inventory improvements cut emissions over the 5-year budget by 12-14 Mt, making the 295 Mt national budget achievable: EPA report pp. 9-10.
- Straight-line path: 21.402 x (1 - 0.25 x 7/12) = 18.28 Mt in 2025. 10% commitment: 19.26 Mt. Target 2030: 16.05 Mt. Computed.
- "Less than a fifth of the distance": 4.7 / 25 = 18.8%.
- EPA projections 26 May 2026: agriculture -4% to -19% by 2030 (2018 base); drivers N fertiliser, fertiliser switching, lower livestock numbers: EPA news release.
- Climate Council 9 Sept 2026: WAM ~19%; 25% achievable if additional diversification measures fully implemented; DAFM time-bound, resourced plan with structured dialogue; 2,527 ha planted in 2025 vs 8,000 target; biomethane far behind: press release.
- "Helped by lower fertiliser use after prices spiked" (2022-23): from EPA's 2023 provisional inventory commentary (fertiliser N fell after the 2022 price spike); not re-checked against that report this time. Verify before publication.
- 25% target set in Sectoral Emissions Ceilings (July 2022) from the 22-30% range in Climate Action Plan 2021; lowest sectoral target. Government documents; known, not re-fetched.
