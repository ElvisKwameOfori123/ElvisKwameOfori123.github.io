# Claim ledger

Compiled 4 October 2026. Public sources only; excluded from the Quarto build.

## Machakos
- Tiffen, Mortimore and Gichuki (1994) More People, Less Erosion; IIED Gatekeeper No. 45 summary: population 240,000 (1930) to 1.4 m (1990), almost sixfold; output per km2 about elevenfold and per head more than threefold, constant 1957 maize values; 1937 vs 1990 photographs; local effort, market access. https://www.iied.org/sites/default/files/pdfs/migrate/6059IIED.pdf . Draft's "2010", "doubled since independence", "tenfold" and export details (Pearce-derived) replaced.

## Demand and climate
- 54% of world population increase to 2050 in SSA; food demand projected to double 2020-2050 (draft said +60-70%, corrected). van Ittersum et al. 2025, PNAS 10.1073/pnas.2423669122 (read via browser).
- Simane, Berhane, Samet et al. (2026) Curr Environ Health Rep 13:8, doi 10.1007/s40572-026-00528-8: warming 1.8-3.0 C; deficits with 1.5%/yr productivity growth: Ethiopia 21%, Kenya 71%, Uganda 60%. Draft's 13-22% yield reduction not re-confirmed; not used.

## Yield gaps
- van Ittersum et al. 2016 PNAS 10.1073/pnas.1610359113: actual yields 15-27% of water-limited potential; ~80% self-sufficiency maintained only with near-complete gap closure, "large, abrupt acceleration"; 80% attainable ceiling "probably too optimistic" in harsh rainfed regions. Draft's "Europe 80% vs Africa 20%, João Vasco Silva professor at Hohenheim" replaced (80% is the model ceiling; Silva is a co-author of the 2025 paper; Hohenheim post not verified).

## 2010-2020 record
- Self-sufficiency 84% -> 92%, population +29%; ten countries: yield 44%, crop shift 22%, area 34%; 2050 Y2020 scenario 46%; cropland expansion detrimental for biodiversity and GHG. van Ittersum et al. 2025.

## Country cases
- Tanzania 235% of rice needs, exporting; Malawi exports: Meshack Malo, FAO, Africa Renewal, 31 March 2026 (his claim).
- Ethiopia wheat: government 23 Mt (2023/24) vs AfDB/USDA 7.5 Mt; private imports 1.4 Mt in 2023. Ethiopian Business Review. Draft's 20,000 ha -> 3.5 Mha and 600,000 -> 128 m quintals are government figures; not used as fact. ICARDA "blueprint" not verified; removed.

## AGRA / AFSA
- AFSA (August 2026) "The Green Revolution Has Failed Africa", quantitative core Timothy Wise (Tufts) analysis of FAO data, 13 AGRA focus countries: fertiliser more than doubled (to 26 kg/ha); cultivated land +46%; staple yields 1.2%/yr vs 1.3% before; undernourished 94.6 m -> 149.6 m (+58%), "almost the same increase recorded across sub-Saharan Africa as a whole"; only Ethiopia and Ghana reduced; Nigeria >half the increase; Malawi +61%. Senegal: hunger halved, staple-yield index +73%, ~14 kg/ha fertiliser. Read in full via browser. Draft's Senegal "millet +85%, sorghum +75%" not found; replaced.
- FAO Investment Centre, 17 June 2026, Soil health and fertilizer: "Decades of subsidy programmes, research and investments have had only limited success..."; integrated, locally adapted strategies.
