## Mechanism
- Verra VVB 101: VVB "contracted ... by a PP". TI-US, "Offsetting Accountability" (2 April 2026), via REDD-Monitor: "Project developers hire and pay auditors".
- VM0048 (late 2023): Verra leads baseline setting, jurisdictional 10-year data, allocated risk maps (Verra page). ICVCM approved VM0048 and ART TREES 2.0 for CCP, 15 Nov 2024; no existing REDD+ credits met CCP (S&P Global).

- Pacajai (VCS-981): 135,105 ha; 10.06 m issued, 7.3 m retired; suspended Sept 2023, possible overlap with state forest without permits; BeZero, Sylvera; WSJ (Yusuf Khan, ~6 March 2026) via REDD-Monitor: >140 corporations retired after suspension; PMI 25,000, Mastercard 11,000+, BlackRock 5,000; <$2/t. Draft's Shell as Pacajai buyer NOT FOUND, removed; ">$50 for high-quality forestry credits" NOT FOUND, removed.
