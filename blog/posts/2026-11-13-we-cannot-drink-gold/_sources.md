# Claim ledger

Compiled 4 October 2026. Public sources only; excluded from the Quarto build. Each item was opened and checked on that date unless marked otherwise.

## Water
- Pra turbidity: Daboase designed 1969 for 54 NTU; Sekyere Hemang 2008 for 500 NTU; peak 13,000 NTU; GWL cut operations by 30%; Prof David Kofi Essumang warned of water imports within 15 years. GNA, 21 March 2024: https://gna.org.gh/2024/03/galamsey-stakeholders-call-for-drastic-coordinated-action-to-save-water-situation/ (secondary, reporting a stakeholder forum). Note: the 15-year warning is Essumang's, not Ghana Water's (corrected from draft).
- Kwanyako: GWL officers showed JoyNews records, 25 NTU (2016) to 94,800 NTU (6 September 2025 visit). Evans Mensah, MyJoyOnline, 18 August 2026: https://www.myjoyonline.com/the-galamsey-war-and-gwls-fictitious-and-inaccurate-rebuttal/ (secondary).
- GWL response: does not hold official river data; internal plant tests are not official monitoring (WRC and EPA mandate). Does not dispute the readings. MyJoyOnline, 18 August 2026: https://www.myjoyonline.com/ghana-water-limited-clarifies-mandate-over-river-turbidity-data/

## Forests and cocoa
- 44 of 288 forest reserves "completely destroyed": Lands Minister Buah, 3 October 2025. Starr FM: https://starrfm.com.gh/galamsey-arrests-prosecution-ghana (draft said "when the current government took office"; corrected to date of statement).
- About 8,900 ha of forest reserves completely degraded: Buah, MyJoyOnline, 15 August 2026 (draft said July; corrected): https://www.myjoyonline.com/galamsey-challenge-is-prosecution-not-arrest-lands-minister/
- Editor's note: the two forest figures measure different things (reserves vs hectares) and sit uneasily together; both are quoted as the minister's statements.
- 100,000+ acres of cocoa destroyed: first cited by the Mankrom Cocoa Cooperative Farmers Association president, Citi Newsroom, 6 September 2024, no stated method: https://citinewsroom.com/2024/09/over-100000-acres-of-cocoa-farms-destroyed-by-illegal-mining-farmers-association/ ; repeated by Samuel Asuman, Regional Manager, Western South CHED, COCOBOD, 14 July 2026, GNA: https://gna.org.gh/2026/07/cocoa-industry-will-collapse-if-urgent-interventions-are-not-made/ . NewsGhana's "Same 100,000 Acre Cocoa Loss Figure Persists" (bot-protected, not read) appears to make the same point.
- 36.5 ha rehabilitated cocoa cut down near Boinso, Aowin: Rev Edwin Afari, Executive Director, CHED, Graphic Online, 27 February 2023: https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/cocoa-industry-under-threat-illegal-miners-destroy-rehabilitated-farms.html (replaces unreadable NewsGhana link).
- COCOBOD officials urging farmers not to sell farms to miners: same GNA report, 14 July 2026.

## Wontumi
- Bernard Antwi Boasiako sentenced 20 July 2026, Accra High Court (Justice Audrey Kocuvie-Tay), 20 years IHL, six counts including assigning mineral rights without ministerial approval; Akonta Mining fined GH¢180,000; offences 2024 at Samreboi. GNA: https://gna.org.gh/2026/07/wontumi-jailed-20-years-for-facilitating-illegal-mining/ . Certified judgment and DUBAWA fact-check not consulted.
- GH¢300,000 per concession and weekly gold royalties (up to 250 g): alleged by Buah at a press briefing, 21 April 2025, from undercover investigations (Aboi and Tano Nimiri reserves). Citi Newsroom: https://www.citinewsroom.com/2025/04/galamsey-govt-raids-akonta-mining-site-orders-revocation-of-lease/
- NPP rejected the verdict; General Secretary Justin Kodua Frimpong: "Chairman Wontumi is a political prisoner"; party said it would appeal (not confirmed as filed). Citi Newsroom, July 2026: https://www.citinewsroom.com/2026/07/npp-rejects-wontumi-conviction-says-verdict-lacked-evidence/ (draft attributed the remark to the Minority; corrected).
