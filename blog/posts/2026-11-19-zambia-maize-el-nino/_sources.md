## Model piece (workflow Stage 4, 1990 to 2015 rule)

- **Model:** Nicole M. Mason and Robert J. Myers, "The Effects of the Food Reserve Agency on Maize Market Prices in Zambia", Food Security Research Project (MSU / Ministry of Agriculture / ACF), Policy Synthesis No. 50, Lusaka, December 2011. https://ageconsearch.umn.edu/record/120766/files/ps_50.pdf
- **Its structure, which this post borrows:**
  1. Key points up front: the agency's scale and dominance of the market.
  2. Introduction: what the FRA does (pan-territorial price, storage, export, sales to millers) and the general perception of its effects.
  3. Evidence: buy price above wholesale; effect on price level; effect on price stability; export losses.
  4. Distribution: who gains and who loses (regressive).
  5. Implications for policy.
- **How this post uses it:** (1) opens with this season's scale (2.7 Mt, 3.1 Mt stocks, supplementary budget), El Niño, Malawi's gap; (2) "What the agency does", citing the synthesis openly; (3) "The price this year"; (4) "Who gains and who pays"; (5) "What the surplus is worth". Their 2011 findings are cited with attribution; no sentences reused.

## Claims
- FRA bought ~2.7 Mt in the 2026 marketing season (>5x 500,000 t target); record stocks 3.1 Mt in September; 4 Nov supplementary budget ZMW19.9bn ($980mn) incl. ZMW14.7bn to pay farmers; ZMW18bn domestic borrowing; Musokotwane quote (3 Nov); little or no new money for FRA next season; IMF resident representative flagged slippages (via Lusaka Times); export ban lifted April; contracts ~1.7 Mt incl. 1.5 Mt for export; 540,000 t to Kenya's Baita Trading, price set per shipment; 200,000 t G2G to Malawi; FRA price ZMW347/50 kg (~$345/t) vs SAFEX white maize Dec ~$232/t on 29 Oct (~49% gap, not directly comparable); South Africa crop 17.69 Mt vs ~12 Mt use; Zimbabwe 24 Bulawayo millers halted in August, $40/t import levy; Malawi ~3.3 Mt vs ~3.5 Mt requirement (agriculture ministry estimates): bne IntelliNews, Brian Kenety, 7 Nov 2026. Secondary (reputable regional business wire); figures attributed in text.
- ZMW14.7bn ≈ $725mn: computed from bne's own conversion (19.9bn = $980mn, ~20.3 ZMW/$).
- El Niño: 63% chance of a very strong event at the Nov-Jan peak (NOAA CPC); southern Africa region of greatest concern; maize planting Oct-Dec 2026, harvest Apr-Jun 2027 coincides with peak: GEOGLAM Crop Monitor special report, 16 June 2026.
- 2011 synthesis: FRA bought 83% of smallholder marketed maize in 2010/11; spending >25% of agricultural expenditure in several years; buy price above wholesale since 2002/03; exports at a trading loss unless subsidised (2007/08, 2010/11); prices raised 19% (Choma) and 17% (Lusaka), July 2003-Dec 2008; coefficient of variation reduced 34-36%; regressive; nearly 50% of smallholders net buyers. Primary (read in full).
- World Bank (2022), "Maize Trade Policies in Zambia: Options for Growth": "export bans have also been imposed following price spikes even during bumper harvest years." Primary, quote verified in PDF.
