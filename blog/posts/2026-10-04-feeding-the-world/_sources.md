# Claim ledger

Compiled 4 October 2026. Public sources only; excluded from the Quarto build.

## Scale
- Our World in Data, Global land for agriculture: "Almost half (44%) of the world's habitable land is used for agriculture"; livestock 80% of agricultural land; 17% of calories, 38% of protein. Draft's "roughly 40 per cent of habitable land" corrected to 44%.
- Crippa et al. 2021, Nature Food, doi 10.1038/s43016-021-00225-9: food systems about a third (34%) of global GHG emissions, 2015.
- Pendrill et al. 2022, Science 377 (Chalmers release): 90-99% of tropical deforestation driven directly or indirectly by agriculture; only 45-65% of deforested land becomes productive agriculture. Draft's "more than 90 per cent" consistent.
- UN WPP 2024: about 9.7 bn in 2050 (draft "nearly 10 billion").

## Grunwald
- Popkin review (Substack): book says farms cover "40 percent, or two-fifths, of our planet's land area"; Searchinger "a hard-core land sparer"; land-sharing gets shorter shrift; asks whether spared land would return to nature. Draft's "land mass the size of Asia plus Europe" not found in sources; removed. "Greatest challenge of the century is not energy but food" not verified as a quote; paraphrased as land being the neglected half of the climate problem.

## Deforestation drivers
- Singh and Persson 2026, Nature Food 7:138-151, doi 10.1038/s43016-026-01305-4. Trase and Nature press release: cattle pasture ~42% of deforestation, 52% of carbon emissions; oil palm + soy 16%; maize, rice, cassava ~11%; earth.com: cocoa, coffee, rubber combined <5%; 122 Mha lost to agriculture 2001-2022; ~41 Gt CO2, ~2 Gt/yr, ~5% of global CO2; earlier estimates more than twice as high. Draft's 42/52 figures confirmed.
- Mazur et al. 2026, PNAS, doi 10.1073/pnas.2521183123 (WRI): 95 Mha non-forest to annual crops + 95 Mha to pasture, 2005-2020; ~4x forest loss to crops and pasture; livestock largest replacement. Draft said 95 Mha to crops only; pasture half added.
- Liu et al. 2026, Nature Food, doi 10.1038/s43016-026-01387-0 (Leiden CML): beef and milk 41% of biodiversity loss and 29% of carbon storage loss on farmland; two-thirds of loss on one-third of land; hotspots listed.

## Debate
- Berkeley Talks, 30 May 2025: Grunwald vs Timothy Bowles (associate professor, ESPM, UC Berkeley); Bowles quote "It's already happening all over the world..."
- Phalan et al. 2011, Science, doi 10.1126/science.1208742 (ScienceDaily): >600 bird and tree species, southwest Ghana and northern India; sparing better for most species; requires active protection.
- Rudel et al. 2009, PNAS (PMC2791618): intensification "not generally accompanied by decline or stasis in cropland area" 1970-2005 except countries with grain imports and set-aside programmes; abandonment needs explicit policy.

## Models
- Gibson, Sundiang, Mason-D'Croz et al., Nature, 15 July 2026, doi 10.1038/s41586-026-10775-2 (IFPRI release): EAT-Lancet 2025 diet + sustainable intensification + 50% waste cut; ag land 6-42% below BAU by 2050; livestock production value -49 to -83%; "historically unprecedented restructuring". Draft's lever list (development in lower-income countries) corrected; "Nature and PNAS studies" narrowed to this Nature study.

## Fuel
- USDA WASDE May 2026: 5.6 bn bushels of ~16 bn to ethanol, 2026/27. Draft's "US and Europe pay farmers to grow corn for ethanol, which drives deforestation in the tropics" overbroad (EU caps crop-based biofuels); narrowed to US demand. Lobby claims removed as unsourced. No link to the unpublished ethanol draft.
