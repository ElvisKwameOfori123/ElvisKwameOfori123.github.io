# Claim ledger

Compiled 4 October 2026. Public sources only; excluded from the Quarto build.

## Nepal
- Glacier collapse 26 August 2026, Bhote Koshi basin; >1,400 dead, thousands missing; hydropower dams breached; Nepal lost >10% of hydropower capacity. The Conversation, Tarun Gopalakrishnan, 18 September 2026 (read in full via browser).
- Foreign Minister Shisir Khanal: "not merely a request for aid or charity but a demand for justice and 'legal and moral liability'" (paraphrase plus quoted phrase, as in The Conversation). Post quotes only "legal and moral liability".
- Damage and loss $2.7 bn; recovery/reconstruction $4.8 bn; $20 m request = 0.7%; Raju Pandit Chhetri "That money is going to be a peanut if it was to be invested into reconstruction." Carbon Brief, 22 September 2026.
- $20 m is the per-proposal cap. Nature editorial, September 2026; Climate Home News, 10 July 2026.

## Fund
- 176 applications, $2.8 bn requested; $822 m pledged, about half paid (Carbon Brief) / 55% delivered (Climate Home). Draft's "$631 m contributions" not supported; replaced with "about half".
- $0 disbursed by September 2026 (Carbon Brief, The Conversation).
- Mid-year board meeting considered four requests, approved none; first approvals deferred to December; 15-20 projects expected. Conversation says June meeting; Climate Home says July, Manila. Post says "mid-year" to avoid the conflict.
- Eight of 26 board members requested an emergency meeting (The Conversation).
- Could run out of money by end 2027 without new contributions (Climate Home, citing separate reporting).
- >3/4 of requests include slow-onset events, e.g. sea walls; 93% involve extreme events (The Conversation, as of June 2026).

## Adaptation finance
- UNEP Adaptation Gap Report 2025: $310 bn/yr by 2035 (modelled), $365 bn (NAP-based); international public adaptation finance $26 bn (2023), $28 bn (2022); gap 12-14x; Glasgow doubling will not be met.
- COP30: "call for efforts" to triple by 2035; no baseline or dollar figure in text; LDC $120 bn proposal not adopted. Carbon Brief. Draft's "agreed to triple to $120 bn" corrected.
- SB64 (Bonn, June 2026): no agreement to reference the tripling goal; deferred to COP31. ACT Alliance, 24 June 2026.
