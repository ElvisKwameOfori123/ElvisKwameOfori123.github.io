- Delays: Reg 2024/3234 (19 Dec 2024); Reg 2025/2650 (19 Dec 2025): 30 Dec 2026 / 30 June 2027; downstream operators collect reference numbers; micro/small primary operators in low-risk countries one-time simplified declaration.
- Information system: read-only from 16 Feb 2026, reopened end June 2026. Draft's "automatically screens every statement for risk" NOT FOUND; removed.

No "no-risk" category (Jones Day via JD Supra). Draft's "did not alter the core legal text" qualified: law not reopened, Annex I amended.
