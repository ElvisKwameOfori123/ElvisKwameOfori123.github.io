## Law and model
- 45Z: emissions rates "shall be adjusted as necessary to exclude any emissions attributed to indirect land use change"; signed 4 July 2025 (H.R. 1, sec. 70521). farmdoc daily, May 2026: https://farmdocdaily.illinois.edu/2026/05/the-clean-fuel-production-tax-credit-45z-introductory-discussion.html ; Stillwater Associates. Statute text itself not opened; quote via farmdoc.
- 45ZCF-GREET update, June 2026; "flawed, unscientific assumptions about biofuels" is Monte Shaw, Iowa Renewable Fuels Association, not DOE (draft attributed to DOE; corrected). Biobased Diesel Daily, 15 June 2026.
- California LCFS: corn ethanol land-use change factor 19.8 gCO2e/MJ since 2016 (30 before). Choices, Q4 2023.
- EPA 2010 RFS2 rule included international ILUC and rejected calls to exclude it for uncertainty. Federal Register 75 FR, 26 March 2010.

## Evidence
- Lark et al. 2022 PNAS 10.1073/pnas.2101084119: corn price +31% (95% CI 5-70), +2.8 Mha corn/yr (8.7%), 2008-2016, CI "no less than gasoline and likely at least 24% higher"; domestic effects only, abroad additional.
- USDA OCE review, 14 December 2022: CRP misclassified as long-term grassland; soil carbon losses an order of magnitude above USDA rates; misapplied response functions.
- Taheripour, Mueller, Kwon, Khanna, Emery, Copenhaver, Wang (Purdue, Illinois, Argonne), May 2022: price effect overstated; idle land mistaken for conversion; N2O double counting.
- Scully et al. 2021 ERL 10.1088/1748-9326/abde08: 51.4 gCO2e/MJ, 46% below gasoline; LUC 3.9 (range -1 to 8.7) gCO2e/MJ. Authors at Environmental Health & Engineering, Harvard Chan, Tufts; no funding statement found in the RFA-hosted copy. Rebutted by Spawn-Lee et al. 2021 (ERL comment) and Lark et al. (2021).
- USDA Lewandrowski et al. 2019 (Biofuels): 39% lower than gasoline, includes revised land-use change estimates. USDA press release, 2 April 2019.

## Aviation
- SAF 0.8% of jet fuel use in 2026 (2.4 Mt). IATA, June 2026.
- ReFuelEU Aviation (Reg. 2023/2405) Art. 4(5): biofuels from food and feed crops excluded from minimum SAF shares. EC FAQ. No stated rationale in FAQ; draft's "precisely because of land-use risk" removed.
