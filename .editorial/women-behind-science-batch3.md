## Important corrections and cautions

- **Jane Ambuko:** current University of Nairobi pages are internally inconsistent on rank. The 2024 professorial inaugural lecture and official programme identify her as Professor of Horticulture, while a departmental staff record still says Associate Professor. Profile uses the professorial status but retains this discrepancy in the ledger.
- **Agnes Mwang’ombe:** older UoN pages contain stale administrative language about being Principal. Do not present that role as current. Current profile uses Professor of Plant Pathology and treats Dean/Principal as prior leadership.
- **Jane Mugo:** the roster entry is valid. The verified researcher is **Jane Wangui Mugo**, Lecturer in Earth and Climate Sciences at UoN, with PhD Meteorology and substantial FAO/IITA climate-risk and crop-modelling work.
- **Dorcas Kalele:** PlanAdapt currently lists her as a Fellow based in Nairobi. Avoid inventing a university appointment or a more permanent institutional title than the current employer page supports.
- **Mary Abukutsa-Onyango:** a current JKUAT leadership page identifies her as Deputy Vice Chancellor for Research, Production and Extension. She is also a Professor of Horticulture. The profile now uses the executive role as the current title rather than treating professorship alone as the current position. Her 2025 Africa Food Prize and ASRIC journal editorship remain verified.
- **Images:** strong images were located for Ambuko, Onyango, Abukutsa-Onyango, Thorn and Amissah, but web availability does not establish republication rights. No image has been copied into the repository in this batch.
