# Editorial audit — 5 October 2026

This audit records a focused check of five recently published EKO Perspectives posts that originated outside the primary EKO drafting session:

- `2026-10-04-africa-feed-itself`
- `2026-10-04-feeding-the-world`
- `2026-10-04-loss-and-damage-money`
- `2026-10-05-carbon-credit-counterfactual`
- `2026-10-05-eudr-cocoa-ghana`

## Findings

All five posts have a sibling `_sources.md` claim ledger and locally stored lead imagery. The ledgers show that unsupported or mismatched figures and quotations were removed or corrected before publication, with explicit notes where sources disagreed or where a claim could not be verified.

No post in this set should be treated as exempt from future fact checking. The most time-sensitive are the EUDR cocoa post, the loss-and-damage post, and any legal or regulatory material in the carbon-credit post. Update only when the underlying legal, regulatory, funding or case status changes materially.

## Standing rule

For externally drafted or heavily assisted posts, publication readiness requires the same checks as any EKO draft: claim ledger, source verification, quotation check, image licence check, originality/proximity review, current-status check, and rendered desktop/phone review.
