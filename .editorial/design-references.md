# Design references for EKO Perspectives

These are standing inspiration references for future visual and storytelling work on kwameofori123.com.

## Africa Sports Alliance
https://www.africasportsalliance.org/

What Elvis likes:
- modern, confident visual presentation
- strong use of photography and people
- clear mission-led storytelling
- sections that move naturally from purpose to programmes to evidence
- clean calls to action without making the page feel like a sales funnel
- a distinctly African identity without visual clutter

## ATF Challenge
https://www.atfchallenge.org/

What Elvis likes:
- contemporary technology/programme aesthetic
- strong hero section
- clear visual hierarchy
- concise sectioning and progression through the story
- programme information presented in a way that feels active rather than bureaucratic
- strong balance between text, visuals, numbers and calls to action

## How to use these references

Use them as inspiration for:
- homepage and landing-page hierarchy
- section rhythm and spacing
- visual storytelling
- programme/project presentation
- cards, statistics and evidence blocks
- photography placement
- calls to action
- mobile-first page flow

Do not copy their exact layouts, wording, branding, colour systems, illustrations or distinctive visual identity.

EKO Perspectives should keep its own editorial identity: research-led, calm, evidence-conscious and personal. The goal is to learn from the clarity and confidence of these sites while preserving EKO's own voice and design system.
