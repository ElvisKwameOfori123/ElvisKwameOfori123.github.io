**Status:** Editorial research complete enough for drafting the first ten profiles. Publication remains blocked by image-rights clearance and owner review.

## Editorial rules

- Profiles use the `people/places behind the science` engine: person + question + place + people + method + consequence.
- Current roles are checked against institutional pages as of 6 October 2026.
- Institutional biographies are treated as starting points, not sole evidence.
- “First publication” is avoided unless a comprehensive bibliographic check establishes it. Profiles use “early publication anchor” where appropriate.
- Publication counts are omitted from prose when databases and institutional pages use different coverage.
- Portraits must be stored locally and must have a reuse basis that satisfies the site image policy. Institutional hosting alone is not permission.
