Elvis Kwame Ofori

Agricultural, land-use and policy modeller

I build economic, spatial, behavioural and computational models to understand how agricultural, climate, land-use and bioenergy policies translate from national objectives into real farms, production systems and places.

The central question is practical: what changes, where does it change, who carries the adjustment, and which policy designs remain workable once implementation is made explicit?
