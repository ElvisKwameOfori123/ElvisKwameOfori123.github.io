Design study

Choose the feel before we rebuild.

These are deliberately different. They are not final websites. Each borrows the design logic of a real theme family so we can decide what feels like you before changing the main site again.

Direction A

Colm / Beautiful Jekyll

Strong personality, dark background, accent colour, round portrait, simple navigation and a more personal academic feel.
Direction B

Minimal academic

PaperMod-style restraint. Very little decoration, excellent reading, publications and writing become the visual focus.
Direction C

Modern scholar

Astro-Scholar style. Modern editorial layout, structured projects and publications, flexible general blog and stronger interaction later.
