# IFT-BioSim

Irish Farm Transition Bioeconomic Microsimulation.

## The question

A national target is one number. The farms that have to meet it are thousands of different businesses, and the same target can cost one of them very little and another a great deal. IFT-BioSim asks how a common national transition objective can create very different economic pressures across heterogeneous farms.

It focuses on **who bears adjustment, who responds, and how policy design changes incidence**.

## What the model represents

The framework links:

- farm structure and livestock adjustment;
- opportunity costs;
- participation decisions;
- income and distributional effects;
- land reallocation; and
- alternative transition-support rules.

## Why it matters

A technically feasible national pathway does not reveal how costly the transition may be for particular farms. IFT-BioSim is designed to make that distribution visible and to test whether alternative compensation or incentive designs change the burden.

## Status

The framework is under active development. Results are not posted here until they are ready for public release.
